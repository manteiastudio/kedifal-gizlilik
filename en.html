Purrtarot — Privacy Policy and Terms of Use

Last updated: 1 August 2026

In short: Purrtarot does not require an account. The name, birth date and city you enter are stored only on your device and are never sent to us or to any server. The app shows Google AdMob ads, and AdMob collects its own advertising identifier. Uninstalling the app deletes all of your data.

1. Who we are

Purrtarot (Turkish edition: KediFal) is an independent mobile application developed by Manteia. In this document, "we", "the app" and "Purrtarot" refer to this application and its developer.

For any question, request or data deletion enquiry: manteiastudio@gmail.com

2. For entertainment only — important notice

All content in Purrtarot — tarot readings, coffee cup readings, daily horoscopes, birth charts and everything else — is provided for entertainment purposes only.

This content does not predict the future, has no scientific basis, and does not constitute medical, psychological, psychiatric, legal, financial or any other professional advice. The texts in the app were not written or reviewed by licensed professionals.

Do not base life decisions on the readings in this app. For matters of health, law or money, consult a qualified professional. If you are struggling emotionally, please reach out to a mental health professional or someone you trust.

By using the app you acknowledge that you treat this content as entertainment and that you are responsible for your own decisions.

3. What data we handle

3.1 Information you enter — stays on your device

To personalise the readings, you may optionally enter the following inside the app:

All of this is stored only in your device's own storage, in the app's private area. It is not sent to us, not uploaded to any server, and not shared with third parties. We cannot see it.

None of it is mandatory. If you leave these blank, the app simply uses general wording.

3.2 Advertising — Google AdMob

The app shows rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. Watching ads is entirely optional; no feature is locked if you choose not to watch them.

AdMob collects certain information from your device in order to serve and measure ads:

  • Advertising ID (AD_ID) — a resettable identifier assigned to your device
  • Device and app information (model, OS version, app version)
  • Approximate location (derived from IP address, country/city level)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, view duration)

We do not collect this data and we have no access to it; it is collected directly by Google and governed by Google's own policies: Google Advertising Policies · Google Privacy Policy

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings: Settings → Google → Ads.

3.3 Notifications

We may send notifications to remind you that your reading is ready, that a daily reward is waiting, or that your streak is at risk. These are scheduled and delivered locally on your device; nothing is sent from a server. Notification permission is optional and can be revoked at any time in your device settings. You can also toggle individual notification types in the app's Settings screen.

3.4 Sharing

If you choose to share a reading, an image is generated on your device and Android's own share sheet opens. You decide which app to send it to.

The shared image never contains your name, birth date or birth place. It shows only the drawn cards, a short quote from the reading, your chosen title, your streak count and your level.

3.5 What we do not collect

  • No accounts, e-mail addresses or passwords
  • No GPS or precise location access
  • No access to contacts, photos, camera, microphone or files
  • No analytics or user-tracking tools
  • We do not sell your data — nothing reaches us, so there is nothing to sell

5. How long data is kept

Data on your device stays until you delete it. There are two ways to do so:

  • In the app: the profile reset option in the Settings screen
  • Completely: uninstalling the app — all data is removed

Since we hold no records about you, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. For AdMob's retention periods, please refer to Google's policies.

6. Age and children

Purrtarot is intended for users aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children and is not listed in a children's category on Google Play.

We do not knowingly collect data from users under 13. In fact we do not collect data from any user on a server. If you believe a child is using the app, simply uninstalling it removes all data along with it.

7. Purchases

This version contains no in-app purchases. Should they be added later, payments will be processed through Google Play; we will never see or store your card details. Refunds are governed by Google Play's own refund policy. This policy will be updated here if that changes.

The in-app currency called "coins" has no real-world monetary value, cannot be exchanged for money, cannot be transferred to other users and is non-refundable. It is reset when the app is uninstalled.

8. Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK) and similar legislation, you have the right to access, correct, delete and object to the processing of your personal data.

In our app these rights are directly in your hands: because all data lives on your device, you can view, change or delete it at any time. We hold no records of you.

For data collected by AdMob you will need to contact Google; the relevant links are in section 3.2.

9. Security

Your data is stored in the app's private area, protected by your device's own security mechanisms. Because we never transmit personal data over the network, there is no transmission-related exposure. However, if your device is rooted or falls into someone else's hands, this data may be accessible; device security is your responsibility.
